# China map presentation assets

`south-china-sea-inset.webp` is a presentation-only monochrome raster derivative of the frozen local standard-map snapshot `china-standard-map.svg` (`GS(2016)1609`) already preserved in this repository.

- Purpose: retain the South China Sea islands locator in the independent 13110 roadshow national view.
- Derivation: crop the source locator inset, convert its printed linework to the roadshow teal, and preserve it as a local transparent WebP.
- Runtime: local asset only; no remote map request.
- Simulation: geographic context only. It does not add simulation provinces, agents, metrics or interaction targets.
