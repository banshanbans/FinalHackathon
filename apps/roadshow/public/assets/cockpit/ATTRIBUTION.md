# New-energy-vehicle cockpit asset

`nev-cockpit-selected.png` is the selected Product Design concept generated for the 13110 roadshow on 2026-08-13. `nev-cockpit-frame.png` is its local production derivative with the center display replaced by a transparent 16:9 aperture.

The interior communicates a premium Chinese new-energy-vehicle cabin without reproducing an automaker logo or presenting endorsement. The live roadshow Earth renders behind the aperture; the asset contains no runtime data or remote dependency.
